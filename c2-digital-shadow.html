C2: the real robot meets its digital shadow

The C2 model is in Isaac Sim, following the real robot’s movement and arm joints.

Today’s progress

I brought the manufacturer’s C2 robot model into Isaac Sim and adapted it to match my robot without a screen. Using the robot’s live state, I connected its movement to the digital model and checked forward motion, turning and all six arm joints.

This is a first digital shadow: the model follows the real robot. Two-way control and validated physics are still ahead.

Next

Check connection stability, bring LiDAR and depth-camera data into the view, and continue investigating a ROS2 node-discovery issue. The existing state-reading connection worked for today’s synchronization test.
