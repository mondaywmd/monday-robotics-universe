03 / FIELD NOTES

First case study: NavBot.

BLENDER OUTPUT · 1280 × 1000

CASE STUDY 001 · ACTIVE

From collisions in Gazebo
to AI-assisted training worlds.

NavBot began as a TurtleBot3 navigation experiment. It could move toward a goal but collided with a pillar directly in its path. That failure led to experiments with residual PPO, control gating, reward shaping, behavior cloning, and generalization.

Policy learning was only part of the problem. Building credible, varied, reproducible environments was another bottleneck. That became the motivation for SimForge Agent.

03 / PRACTICE WITH AN AI AGENT

Explain it back after learning it.

I also suggest using an AI agent to generate practice questions. Here is a reusable prompt:

Using the official NVIDIA OpenUSD learning modules I provide, create 10 original practice questions mixing concepts, scenario analysis and code reading. Ask one at a time and wait for my answer. Explain each answer and distractor, link verifiable official references, and summarize my weak areas at the end.

AI exercises are not official mock exams or exam questions. Verify answers against documentation or small experiments. These are personal study suggestions, not a pass guarantee; NVIDIA is the authority on courses and exams.
