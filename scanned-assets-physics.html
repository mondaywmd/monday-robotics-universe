DEVLOG · 2026.09.25 · SCAN / BLENDER / ISAAC SIM

Turn household objects
into digital assets with collision geometry.

Scan household objects, clean and reconstruct them, then import to Isaac Sim. Use 200 falling balls to inspect whether collision shapes match the visible forms.

00 / THE ASSET LIBRARY

Build an asset library.
Keep using the modeled assets in experiments.

Build a reusable household-obstacle library, not only a demonstration. Each scan and model becomes an independent asset for different rooms, layouts and robot tests.

Real objects → reference scans → agent + Blender cleanup → USD assets → Isaac Sim validation → scene reuse. Scans capture appearance; Blender repairs geometry, dimensions and materials; USD transports assets; Isaac Sim tests contact and sensing. Feed validation back into modeling—successful export is not completion.

SOURCE

Keep original input

Keep the original scan separately for review and reprocessing. Record physical dimensions, photographs and scan limitations together.

ASSET

Independently managed per item

Each asset has Blender and USD files, previews and notes, with visual and collision geometry separated. Reference source assets into test scenes; keep experimental collider overrides in the test layer.

EVIDENCE

Keep validation with the asset

Record dimensions, reconstruction methods, collision choices, passed tests and remaining limits. A ball contacting an asset does not prove a robot can navigate around it.

The current local asset library is called simforge_assets , organized by object raw / blender / usd / previews , supporting notes and asset information. Isaac Sim stores referencing scenes, scripts and recordings. Large models and textures remain local; the website presents experiments and GitHub tracks code and lightweight metadata. This is a growing index, not an online asset repository.

SimForge asset creation connects to Omniverse validation: build credible individual assets, compose navigation scenes, then expand layouts and training. The asset library remains reusable throughout.

01 / REAL OBJECTS

Scan, then check physical dimensions.

Scan a book, dumbbell, shoe and chair into separate GLBs. Check missing faces, floor fragments and scale. Approximate physical dimensions constrain modeling; the scans are not precision measurements.

Assets Dimensional constraints Method

02 / BLENDER REBUILD

Use scans as references; deliver complete geometry.

The scanned book had no bottom. Rebuild a closed book using the scan and measured dimensions: the front texture comes from the scan; the back, edges and page block are reconstructed.

Remove scanned ground, repair seams and holes, voxel-remesh and bake the shoe textures. The left shoe and its texture are mirrored from the right, not independently scanned; small holes are simplified. The chair is rebuilt to dimensions with new wood and cushion materials.

03 / SIM-READY

Define physical boundaries as well as appearance.

Assets use meters, Z-up and a base at Z=0, with separate visuals/colliders. Book: box; dumbbell: segmented convex hulls; chair: multiple convex parts preserving gaps. Keep scans, Blender sources, USDs, previews and notes separately.

These assets are initially static obstacles without arbitrary masses. A separate scene checks ball contacts and raycasts through selected shoe and chair gaps. This validates sampled locations, not every collision surface.

At the robot's 18.2 cm scan plane, missing the lower book, dumbbell and shoes is expected; the chair is detected. Lowering the scan detects the low objects. Physical collision does not imply visibility to a particular horizontal scan.

04 / DEBUG THE COLLISION

Why does the ball stick? Why can't it enter the shoe?

Balls initially stopped too quickly, sometimes mid-bounce. Setting both Sleep Threshold and Stabilization Threshold to 0 restored continuous motion. Their effects were not isolated, so the cause is not assigned to either alone.

Increase ball restitution to 0.68 with max combine mode; static friction 0.28 and dynamic friction 0.20. Low damping permits continued rolling after bounce. These are demonstration settings, not universal training defaults.

I asked whether convex hulls prevented balls entering shoes. Each shoe's three hulls preserved the inter-shoe gap but filled its opening. A test-layer override to static triangle mesh lets rays and balls enter the cavity. Source assets remain unchanged; detailed colliders are local to this test.

05 / TWO HUNDRED BALLS

From uniform drops to varied collisions.

Progress from 24 to 100 to 200 balls. Vary initial positions, heights and linear/angular velocities under a fixed camera. Use 188 balls of radius 2.6 cm and 12 of radius 1.6 cm aimed into shoe openings with low lateral speed. All motion is physics-driven, without keyframes or snapping.

Movie Capture exported 360 PNGs, encoded into MP4 with FFmpeg and decode-checked. The built-in encoder had previously stalled; frame sequences preserve recoverable source images. Earlier 24-ball and 100-ball versions remain local.

06 / REPLAY

Preserve the experiment and the operating procedure.

In Isaac Sim, open the local asset_ball_shower_200.usda , switch to /World/ShowerCamera , press Play to observe and Stop to reset. Enable collider visualization for the shoe; inspect the balls' Physics Material for settings. Save edits before rerunning the recording script in Script Editor.
