Result: 5/5 mock collections

Four paper wads and one water bottle collected in about 78.2 seconds of simulation time. The selected kitchen framing keeps a little of the window visible and avoids blurry foreground regions.

The workflow

GS supplies the kitchen appearance. The exported World Labs collision mesh and a stable floor support Isaac Sim physics. Reuse RGB detection, depth localization, reachable parking and LiDAR avoidance; remember litter after multi-frame confirmation, stop, then request mock collection.

Bring recorded robot motion into Blender and composite it with GS. Refine color handling, ground shadows and occlusion, and reuse the selected transparent bottle variant A with its blue-and-white label. The final presentation uses a fixed camera.

Result and scope

5/5 simulated items collected, with a maximum monitored contact force of 0 N. Two collection requests caused by background false detections were rejected; no environment objects were removed.

This is simulation-only mock collection, not physical grasping. Robot localization uses simulator ground truth; navigation targets come from online RGB and depth detection. This run does not establish full patrol coverage. GS still blurs or distorts in some views, so we select acceptable presentation angles.
